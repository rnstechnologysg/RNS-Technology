# Company Overview

RNS Technology Pte. Ltd. is a Singapore-based railway engineering company specializing in rolling stock engineering, maintenance, modification, testing & commissioning, TCMS support, Platform Screen Door (PSD) engineering, and Defect Liability Period (DLP) support.

The company partners with railway operators, rolling stock manufacturers, maintenance contractors, engineering consultants, and system integrators to deliver safe, reliable, and high-quality engineering services throughout the lifecycle of railway assets.

The company focuses on technical excellence, quality workmanship, engineering innovation, and workplace safety.
