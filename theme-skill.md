======================================================
DESIGN PRINCIPLE
======================================================

The capability statement already communicates
technical excellence, safety, quality, and reliability.

The website should amplify those qualities through
clean layouts, restrained color usage, premium typography,
large engineering icons, subtle motion, and authentic
railway visuals rather than decorative effects.

The user should feel that they are visiting a global
railway engineering company, not a generic SaaS product.
